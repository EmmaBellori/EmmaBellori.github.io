PRIVACY · ANALYTICS · CONTACT

Privacy, clearly explained.

Emma Bellori Books is an independent author website hosted on GitHub Pages. You can browse the site without accepting optional analytics, and you can change your analytics choice at any time.

At a glance

Google Analytics is optional. Amazon purchases happen on Amazon. Upcoming-book preferences use a pseudonymous visitor identifier. Privacy questions can be sent directly to Emma Bellori Books.

OPTIONAL ANALYTICS

Google Analytics 4

If you choose Accept analytics, this website loads Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-PJDVFZV1QG) to understand how the site is used and improve the reading experience.

Depending on your activity, analytics may include page visits, book-page interactions, interior-preview opens and outbound clicks to Amazon. Google may process device, browser, approximate location and usage information and may use cookies or similar identifiers. If you decline analytics, this website does not load Google Analytics.

YOUR CHOICE

Analytics consent

You can accept or reject optional analytics when the consent panel appears. The Privacy settings control in the footer lets you revisit that choice later.

If you reject analytics after previously accepting it, the page reloads to stop further analytics requests from this site. You can also clear existing site cookies through your browser settings.

AMAZON

Books and purchases

Links to books open Amazon's website. Amazon's own privacy, cookie and purchase practices apply once you leave this website.

Emma Bellori Books does not receive your Amazon payment details or the personal information you provide during an Amazon purchase through this website.

COMING SOON VOTING

Optional upcoming-book preferences

Visitors may optionally support one upcoming book per series and change their selection later. A randomly generated browser identifier is stored locally so the site can recognize the visitor's choices. Before a preference is stored in the Supabase database, the identifier is pseudonymized using a keyed hash.

The site does not publicly display individual preferences or voting totals. Clearing browser storage may reset the local identifier and the selections shown on your device.

To help prevent automated abuse, Cloudflare Turnstile may process technical data such as IP address and browser information. Server-side rate limiting uses pseudonymized network identifiers for security and abuse prevention.
