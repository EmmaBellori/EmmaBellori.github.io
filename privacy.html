Privacy & Analytics

Emma Bellori Books is an independent author website hosted on GitHub Pages. You can browse the website without accepting analytics.

Optional Google Analytics

If you choose “Accept analytics”, we load Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-PJDVFZV1QG) to understand site visits, pages viewed, and clicks on Amazon links. Google may process device, browser, approximate location and usage data, and may use cookies or similar identifiers. If you decline, our site does not load Google Analytics.

Your choice

Use the “Privacy settings” control in the footer to change your preference. Rejecting after acceptance reloads the page to stop further analytics requests from this site. To clear existing Google Analytics cookies, you may also clear this site's cookies in your browser.

Amazon links

Amazon links open Amazon's website, where Amazon's own privacy practices apply. We do not receive your purchase details through this website.

Hosting and contact

GitHub Pages may process technical connection data to serve this website independently of your analytics choice. Before publishing a final legal privacy notice, the website operator should add their identity and a usable contact address, and review the applicable legal requirements.
