TRY IT TOGETHER

Little ideas for families

Simple ways to continue the conversation or creativity beyond the page.

  • Ask: “What did you learn from trying?”
  • Share a small mistake you made and what helped you move forward.
  • Celebrate effort and trying again, not just getting it right.
