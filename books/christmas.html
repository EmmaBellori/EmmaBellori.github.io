Christmas Kawaii

Coloring & Activity Book for Kids

Smiling snowmen, cuddly winter animals, Santa and sweet gingerbread friends — a cozy screen-free Christmas activity book created for kids ages 4–10.

Bold outlines, rounded characters and generous coloring spaces make the illustrations inviting, while short English instructions keep the activities easy to follow.

INSIDE THE BOOK

Coloring, puzzles and cozy Christmas play

36 Christmas pictures to color plus 14 playful activities, including mazes, counting, matching, patterns, tracing, a word search, connect-the-dots, simple addition, picture sudoku and creative pages.

The large 8.5 × 11-inch format gives children generous room to color. Each of the 51 content pages has a completely blank reverse, for 102 pages in total.
