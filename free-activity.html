My Little Brave Steps

My name: ___________________________________

1. Name a feeling

Draw a face or choose a word for how you feel today. Happy, nervous, curious and frustrated are all welcome.

2. Try one small thing

What is something you could try again, with help or in a new way? Draw or write one little step.

3. Share a kind word

Think of one kind sentence for yourself or someone else. Say it out loud together, then write or draw it here.

For the grown-up: Listen without correcting. Thank your child for sharing. You can model a feeling or a kind word of your own.
