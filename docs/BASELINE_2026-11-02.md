# Growth baseline — 2 November 2026

This baseline is intentionally small: the site has only recently begun receiving measurable Google impressions. It exists to make later growth comparisons honest.

## GA4: 27 Oct – 1 Nov 2026

Observed sessions in the connected GA4 report:
- Direct: 4 sessions
- Organic Search: 11 sessions
- Organic Social: 1 session
- Total across reported channel rows: 16 sessions
- Page/screen views across those rows: 42
- Amazon click key events in the reported rows: 0

Do not sum the per-row Active Users values as a unique-user total because the same user can appear across rows/dates.

## Search Console: first observed query impressions

Examples already appearing:
- `nobody wants to play with me` → book page, positions observed around 4–6
- `i can't sleep without you story` → book page, position observed around 9
- `i can't sleep without you novel` → book page, position observed around 4
- `losing in a game` → losing guide, position observed around 49
- `loses a game` → losing guide, position observed around 73

The connected Search Console source is still the legacy URL-prefix property under `/emma-bellori-books/`. The site itself has migrated to the root domain and redirects legacy URLs, but root-property reporting should be connected separately for a clean long-term baseline.

## Interpretation

The sample is too small for performance conclusions. The useful signal is that Google has begun testing both book pages and problem/solution guides in search results. The next goal is to increase the number of relevant search entry pages while measuring which ones produce downstream Amazon clicks and mailing-list signups.
