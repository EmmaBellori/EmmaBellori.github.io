EMMA BELLORI BOOKS — AGGIORNAMENTO PAGINE LIBRI

1. Apri https://github.com/emmabellori/emma-bellori-books
2. Carica tutti i file di questo ZIP mantenendo la struttura delle cartelle (inclusa books/ e images/).
3. Conferma le sostituzioni di index.html e app.js; aggiungi book-pages.css, sitemap.xml, robots.txt e la cartella books/.
4. Attendi GitHub Pages e visita https://emmabellori.github.io/emma-bellori-books/books/mistakes.html
5. Controlla che copertine e pulsanti Amazon funzionino.
6. In Pinterest usa gli URL delle singole pagine, riportati sotto.

I Hate Making Mistakes : https://emmabellori.github.io/emma-bellori-books/books/mistakes.html
Nobody Wants to Play With Me! : https://emmabellori.github.io/emma-bellori-books/books/play.html
The Amazing Bug Detective : https://emmabellori.github.io/emma-bellori-books/books/bugs.html
When I Lose, I Get REALLY Mad : https://emmabellori.github.io/emma-bellori-books/books/mad.html
Christmas Kawaii : https://emmabellori.github.io/emma-bellori-books/books/christmas.html
Halloween Kawaii : https://emmabellori.github.io/emma-bellori-books/books/halloween.html
